For Simba, no live page (including images/media/about/find-simba) discloses ABV, package size, price, or MRP in text; the /our-beers/ sub-pages (light [titled 'Lager'], strong, wit, stout) show only marketing copy, tasting-note flavor tags, and bottle product photography, with no numeric spec data. I supplemented Simba with a third-party price-aggregator site (liquorsprice.com) via WebFetch for illustrative ABV/size/price ranges, but I flag these explicitly as unverified secondary-source claims, not confirmed against the brand's own site, per the task's anti-fabrication rule. For Toit, ABV values ARE published per-beer on the brand's own site (verified in raw HTML) but package size and MRP are NOT — the site only makes a generic, brand-wide claim ('available in partner bars and at an MRP store near you') without SKU-level packaging/pricing detail, so I omitted those fields rather than guessing. For Geist, package size (500ml crowler) and Karnataka MRP ARE published per-SKU for exactly 4 beers (James Blond, Uncle Dunkel, Kamacitra, Witty Wit) at https://www.geist.in/retail, explicitly scoped to 'select retail stores in Bangalore' (Karnataka) and Pondicherry — this is the strongest, most directly verified retail-SKU dataset found across all four brands. For Arbor, the brand's own homepage explicitly and unambiguously states its canned/retail beers are sold ONLY in Goa, while its Bangalore (Karnataka) presence is taproom/draft-only — this is a clean, well-documented example of the retail-vs-taproom distinction the task asked me to enforce, so I included those two canned beers but flagged them as NOT Karnataka retail SKUs. I did not attempt browser-based JS rendering (the MCP browser tool was unavailable/timed out in this environment) but was able to get complete data via raw HTML fetch + regex/JSON-LD parsing for Geist and Arbor (both JS-framework sites, Webflow and Wix respectively) because critical content was present in server-rendered HTML/structured data. I could not access barcodes/GTINs, calorie counts, or product image URLs for most SKUs from these sources, and have omitted those fields entirely rather than fabricate them.
