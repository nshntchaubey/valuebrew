# Catalog Extraction Source: Carlsberg India (carlsbergindia.com) and Carlsberg Group global brand catalog (carlsberggroup.com)

- Source URL: https://carlsbergindia.com/our-products/beers-you-love/ and https://carlsberggroup.com/beers-you-love/
- SKUs extracted: 10
- Access notes: Successfully accessed and directly loaded (both via WebFetch and via raw curl to capture full HTML for grep verification) the following real, working URLs on carlsbergindia.com, all returning HTTP 200: the homepage (https://carlsbergindia.com/), /our-products/beers-you-love/, /our-products/other-products/, and individual product pages at /products/carlsberg/carlsberg-smooth/, /products/carlsberg/carlsberg-elephant/, /products/tuborg/tuborg-green/, /products/tuborg/tuborg-strong/, /products/tuborg/tuborg-classic-with-scotch-malts/, /products/tuborg/tuborg-ice-draft/, and /products/1664/1664-blanc/. These pages give beer type/style, country of origin, launch year, brief description, and SKU/package sizes -- but NONE of them publish ABV values anywhere on the India site.

For ABV, I cross-checked the global Carlsberg Group brand catalog at carlsberggroup.com (canonical path /beers-you-love/; the /brands/ path redirects there). I paged through all 15 pages of its ~705-result worldwide catalog via curl (?p=1 through ?p=15, all HTTP 200) and located name-matching entries with explicit Beer Type / ABV / Brand Origin fields in the HTML for: Carlsberg Elephant, Carlsberg Smooth Draught (name partially matches 'Carlsberg Smooth'), Tuborg Green, Tuborg Strong, Tuborg Classic With Scotch Malts, and 1664 Blanc.

IMPORTANT CAVEAT: the global catalog is NOT India-specific -- matches were made by product name and cross-checked against 'Brand Origin' or an India-flagged image filename where possible. For 'Tuborg Classic With Scotch Malts' the matched entry's image URL contains 'in_tuborg-classic-bottle.png', a fairly strong signal it IS the India SKU (marked High confidence). For 'Carlsberg Smooth', the only name match was 'Carlsberg Smooth Draught' with Brand Origin = Malaysia, conflicting with the India product -- so its ABV was NOT reported as a fact for the India product, only noted as an unconfirmed possibility. For Tuborg Strong, the global entry's origin (Denmark) conflicts with the India site's stated India origin -- flagged as a discrepancy, ABV kept as Medium confidence. Tuborg Ice Draft could not be found by name anywhere across all 15 pages of the global catalog, so its ABV is reported as unknown rather than fabricated.

No retailer/excise site (e.g., Karnataka MRP listings) was checked since no state was specified in the task and no such source was found via the searches performed -- karnataka_mrp, barcode, gtin, and calories fields are omitted entirely for all products since no verified source was found. No login, CAPTCHA, or robots.txt block was encountered on either domain. Every fact reported is either (a) directly read from a fetched page (High confidence, unambiguous) or (b) cross-matched by name between two real pages, explicitly labeled as inference with Medium confidence and the reasoning/discrepancy stated in each 'last_verified_note'. No fact was invented; unverifiable fields (image_url, calories, barcode, gtin, karnataka_mrp) are omitted or marked unknown rather than guessed.
