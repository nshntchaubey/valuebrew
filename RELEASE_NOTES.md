# ValueBrew 1.0 — Release Notes

## What ValueBrew does

ValueBrew helps you pick a beer that's actually worth your money, based
on what you tell it matters to you — not just the sticker price.

## What you can do

- **Get a recommendation.** Tell ValueBrew your budget, and it picks the
  beer with the best real value for that money — cost measured per unit
  of alcohol, not just the price on the shelf.
- **Narrow it down by style.** If you'd rather stick to a lager, a
  stout, or whatever you're in the mood for, refine your recommendation
  by style — at any point, and you can always clear it and go back.
- **Get an honest answer when there's a genuine tie.** If two or more
  beers are truly, exactly equal in value within your budget, ValueBrew
  says so — and shows you both — instead of picking one for you
  arbitrarily.
- **See the full picture on any recommended beer.** Price, size,
  package, alcohol content, value score, and how recently the price was
  checked.
- **Check if you were charged fairly.** Enter what you actually paid for
  a specific beer, and ValueBrew tells you whether that matches the
  legal reference price — and flags it plainly if it's higher.
- **Plan ahead.** If you're thinking about a future purchase rather than
  buying right now, use the "I'm planning ahead" option — ValueBrew will
  remind you throughout that prices and availability might have changed
  by the time you actually buy.

## Every recommendation is explained

ValueBrew never just hands you a beer name. Every recommendation, every
tie, and every price check comes with a plain-language explanation of
why — what mattered, and what the app is actually sure of.

## What ValueBrew doesn't do (yet)

- **No search or browse.** Right now, ValueBrew only reaches a specific
  beer through a recommendation — there's no way to look up a beer you
  already have in mind by name.
- **No side-by-side comparison** between two or more beers you pick
  yourself.
- **No preferences beyond budget and style** — strength, size, and
  brand preferences aren't supported yet.
- **No accounts, no sign-in, nothing saved between visits.** Every
  recommendation starts fresh. This is a deliberate choice, not a
  missing feature — see Privacy below.

## Privacy

No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Nothing you
enter — your budget, your style preference, or a price you're checking
— is stored anywhere beyond the moment you're using it. Full details:
[`privacy_policy.md`](privacy_policy.md).
